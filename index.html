Gameplay & Mechanics

Watermelon Game

A casual physics puzzle game: drop and combine matching fruits to score points and beat your high score, includes a multiplayer mode. Built solo, released on Steam in November 2023 and also published on the Microsoft Store.

  • Three skills that change up the gameplay
  • Multiplayer with its own stone fruit mechanic
  • Golden fruit and combo counter
  • Expanded the original formula with my own ideas

Systems & Balancing

Rogue Deck In development

My current project: a multiplayer card game, simple like UNO but with deck building depth similar to YuGiOh and roguelike elements. Not released yet, but you can download the current build.

  • Every card in the deck has a unique effect
  • A second deck creates a roguelike progression, with further combination possibilities
  • Balancing from logged playtests, with a downloadable spreadsheet
  • Interconnected card effects and strategic combinations
  • Effect strength comparisons

Core Loop & Controls

Queue-Connect

A mobile arcade game: repair robots on a speeding conveyor belt by tapping the right parts before they roll away. Started as our Global Game Jam 2020 entry, then polished with a fellow School of Games student and published on the Google Play Store (no longer listed). Still available on itch.io.

  • Redesigned a PC jam prototype for two-thumb landscape play
  • Rising belt speed, decoy parts and risk/reward buffs
  • Short score runs built for "one more try" mobile sessions
